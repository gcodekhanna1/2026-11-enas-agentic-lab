The goal

You're going after summer internships, and keeping track of it all gets overwhelming fast — which ones you care about, whether you've applied, who you've spoken to, what you owe them, and what you'd have to change in your resume. You want one place that holds all of it.

The five of you are after different things. Whatever you build has to work for all of you — not just for whoever is typing.

Discovery: 30 minutes
All five questions, then hand it to Claude. The rest of the session is yours to build and iterate — so don't polish this page, finish it.

Answer these about your own experience. Don't design anything yet — just describe how it actually goes. Everyone at the table answers; differences are useful.

1Where do you normally find out about internships?

Public job sites, the Yale career site, a professor, a friend who already works there? Go around the table.

2What frustrates you most about applying?

Be specific. The last time it went badly, what actually went wrong?

3What do you wish you could remember about each one — before you apply, and after?

Deadlines, links, who you emailed, where you stand, what they said.

4How much do you rewrite your resume or cover letter for each application?

What does that actually involve, step by step?

5What do you find out too late?

List those first. Then: so what should this thing tell you, and when should it tell you?
